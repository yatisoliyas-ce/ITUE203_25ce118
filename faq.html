Frequently asked questions

Account & registration

How do I create a StudentHub account?

Go to the Register page and fill in your personal and academic details, then set a password.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Use the "Forgot your password?" link on the Login page, or contact support.

Can I change my department after registering?

Yes. Visit your Profile page and update your academic details, or contact the registrar's office.

Using the portal

Where can I see my attendance and courses?

Your Dashboard shows enrolled courses, attendance percentage, and pending tasks.

How do I find out about upcoming events?

The Events page lists all upcoming campus events, workshops, and deadlines.

How do I report a problem or suggest an improvement?

Use the Feedback page to submit comments about StudentHub.

Accessibility

Is StudentHub keyboard accessible?

Yes. Every page includes a skip-to-content link, semantic landmarks, and labelled form fields so the site can be navigated by keyboard and screen readers.

Who do I contact about an accessibility issue?

Reach out through the Contact page and select "Technical support" as the subject.
